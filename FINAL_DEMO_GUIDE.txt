1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & PROBLEM STATEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Municipal procurement in India currently suffers from disclosure fragmentation 
across isolated systems:
* Track A: Annual Municipal Budget Books published by the Accounts Department 
  (e.g., Accounts Code RI11A101 for Water Supply distribution).
* Track B: E-tendering notices published on official portals (mahatenders.gov.in) 
  and mirrored across state/national aggregator sites.

Discrepancies frequently arise due to OCR errors, typographical slips in budget 
books, synchronization lags between official portals and mirrors, and schedule 
of rates misclassifications.

THE SOLUTION:
A continuous, mathematically verified reconciliation engine engineered under 
the Harsh Manjramkar SDLC Flow:
1. Complete Type-State Safety: Invalid transitions are mathematically 
   unrepresentable at compile time using Rust with zero unsafe code.
2. Discrete Integer Currency: All arithmetic uses exact Indian Paise (1 INR = 
   100 Paise), eliminating IEEE-754 floating-point rounding drift.
3. Polite Crawling & Cryptographic Provenance: Rate-limited crawling with SHA-256 
   pre-image binding for every raw payload.
4. Non-Defamatory Policy: Enforces neutral technical terminology backed by 
   pre-approved innocent explanations.
5. Zero-Terminal Command Center: Complete interactive demonstration executed 
   entirely through a high-performance, zero-scroll web interface.

SCOPE BOUNDARY:
Strictly scoped to PMC Accounts (budget reference data) and Water Supply (tenders). 
Election procurement is explicitly out of scope because municipal elections in 
Pune fall under the jurisdiction of the District Collector (RFCP code) rather 
than the municipal corporation budget (PMCP code).

4. MATHEMATICAL RIGOR & HIGH-ASSURANCE ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

A. DISCRETE FIXED-POINT CURRENCY ARITHMETIC (ZERO FLOAT DRIFT)
Standard IEEE-754 double precision floating point introduces representation 
error (e.g., 0.1 + 0.2 = 0.30000000000000004). In municipal financial audits, 
this is unacceptable.
* We implement a discrete Paise algebra field: 1 INR = 100 Paise, 1 Crore = 
  1,000,000,000 Paise.
* All additions, subtractions, and tolerance thresholds are computed as 64-bit 
  integers (i64), guaranteeing zero arithmetic drift.

B. COMPILE-TIME TYPE-STATE AUTOMATA IN RUST
In standard web frameworks, a junior engineer can accidentally generate an audit 
report for an unverified notice.
In our Rust kernel (crates/pmc_core), state transitions are strictly linear:
    RawTenderNotice 
      --(.verify())--> VerifiedNotice (SHA-256 pre-image check)
      --(.match_budget())--> MatchedTender (Correlation proof)
      --(.generate_dossier())--> ReconciliationDossier
Calling .verify() consumes RawTenderNotice by value; invalid pipeline transitions 
are physically impossible to compile.

C. ROBUST STATISTICAL HAMPEL FILTER (MAD)
Public data exhibits heavy skew and extreme outliers. Traditional Z-Scores based 
on mean and standard deviation break down because the outlier inflates the mean.
We use the Hampel Identifier based on Median Absolute Deviation (MAD):
    MAD = Median( | X_i - Median(X) | )
    Modified Z = (0.6745 * | X_target - Median(X) |) / MAD
This detects extreme anomalies reliably even in small sample sizes.

D. CODEQL CWE SECURITY HARDENING
* CWE-89 (SQL Injection): Strictly parameterized SQL queries; string 
  formatting is prohibited and blocked by Semgrep AST rules.
* CWE-79 (Cross-Site Scripting): The UI state engine passes all strings through 
  HTML entity escaping before DOM insertion.
* CWE-22 (Path Traversal): Storage paths enforce strict alphanumeric ID 
  whitelisting and verify path containment via is_relative_to().
* CWE-20 (Improper Input Validation): All API payloads are validated against 
  strongly typed Pydantic contracts.

6. EVALUATOR FAQ & DEFENSIVE TALKING POINTS
================================================================================

Q1: Why is Election procurement not reconciled alongside Water Supply?
A1: According to the statutory framework of municipal administration in 
    Maharashtra, municipal elections in Pune are administered directly by the 
    District Collector's Election Branch. Tenders for polling booths, webcasting, 
    and logistics use the tender portal code prefix "RFCP" (Collector Pune), 
    whereas Pune Municipal Corporation tenders use "PMCP". Furthermore, the PMC 
    Annual Budget has no municipal election line item. Confusing the two would 
    be a statutory error.

Q2: How does the crawler prevent overloading government infrastructure?
A2: Our polite crawler (src/pmc_recon/ingest/polite_crawler.py) enforces a strict 
    2.0-second delay per host, declares an identifying User-Agent header with 
    contact info, and uses HTTP ETag / If-None-Match conditional headers to 
    eliminate duplicate bandwidth.

Q3: Why is neutral language legally critical in procurement auditing?
A3: Accusatory words such as "fraud" or "corruption" create legal exposure and 
    defamation liabilities. In public procurement audits, discrepancy flags must 
    always use objective descriptors ("valuation variance", "arithmetic 
    discrepancy") and provide pre-approved innocent explanations (synchronization 
    lag, OCR entry error) to enable fair administrative adjudication.
